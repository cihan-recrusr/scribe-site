Scribe: Meeting Notes Support

Scribe: Meeting Notes records your meetings — your microphone and the other side playing through your Mac — and turns them into a transcript labelled Me and Others, plus a summary with topics, decisions and action items.

Contact: cnbabatas@gmail.com — please include your macOS version and the app version (Settings shows it).

Getting started

  1. Add your OpenAI API key. Open Settings (⌘,), paste a key from https://platform.openai.com/api-keys and press Save. The key is kept in your macOS Keychain. Usage is billed to your OpenAI account.
  2. Start a recording from the menu bar icon or the main window.
  3. Allow the permissions macOS asks for the first time: Microphone, and System Audio Recording (so the other side of the call is captured).
  4. Stop, then press Process on the recording. The first time, the app asks whether it may send recordings to OpenAI; nothing is uploaded until you allow it.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an OpenAI account?

Yes, to transcribe and summarize. You can record without a key; processing needs one. The app has no subscription of its own — you pay OpenAI directly for what you use.

The other side of my call is silent.

  • Check System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording, and make sure the app is allowed under System Audio Recording Only.
  • Turn off Echo cancellation in Settings. While it is on, macOS does not deliver system audio to the app.

My own voice is very quiet.

Some headsets arrive with a very low input level. The app raises a too-low input level when a recording starts and tells you when it did. You can also adjust it in System Settings → Sound → Input.

Me and Others are mixed up.

Use headphones. Without them, the far side plays out of your speakers and is picked up by your microphone too.

Where are my recordings?

Inside the app's own container on your Mac. They never leave your Mac unless you process them. Delete a recording from its detail view; deleting the app removes them all.

How do I stop sending recordings to OpenAI?

Leave Process recordings automatically off, use Ask Again Before Sending in Settings to withdraw permission, or remove your API key.

Is recording people legal?

That depends on where you and the other participants are. Some places require everyone's consent. Make sure you are allowed to record before you do.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy. In short: the developer collects nothing; recordings go only to OpenAI, only with your key and only after you allow it.
