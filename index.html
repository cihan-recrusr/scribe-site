Scribe: Meeting Notes

Record, transcribe, summarize. Scribe records your meetings on your Mac, your microphone and the other side of the call, and turns them into a transcript that tells you who said what, plus a summary you can act on.

Coming soon on the Mac App Store

What it does

  • Two sides, clearly labelledScribe records your voice and your Mac's system audio as two separate channels, so the transcript marks every line as Me or Others.
  • Summaries that get to the pointEvery recording gets a title, a short summary, the topics covered, the decisions made, the action items with their owners, and the questions left open.
  • Works with any call appIf you can hear it on your Mac, Scribe can record it. No bots joining your calls, no plug-ins.
  • Stays on your MacRecordings, transcripts and summaries are stored locally. There is no Scribe account and no Scribe server. Nothing leaves your Mac until you ask for it to be processed.

Transcription and summaries are powered by OpenAI, using your own API key, stored in your Keychain. You pay OpenAI directly for what you use; Scribe has no subscription. Before the first upload, Scribe tells you what will be sent and asks for your permission.

Requirements

  • macOS 14.4 or later
  • An OpenAI API key (platform.openai.com)
  • Microphone and System Audio Recording permissions

Recording laws differ between countries; some require everyone's consent. Make sure you are allowed to record before you start.
